KM 1Who I am

The short version

I build decision material from data and get organisations to actually use it. Since 2007 I have moved across investment analysis, valuation, controlling and business development, the last nine years at Jernhusen, the company behind Sweden's railway stations.

The industry has been real estate. The toolbox is universal: data, finance, pedagogy, and a stubborn urge to make the difficult simple. The best decision material is not the thickest, it is the clearest. Numbers should tell a story, otherwise they are just numbers.

A close friend once described me as a naive realist. I checked the numbers and he is right.
KM 2The route

Prague → Stockholm, the long way

A career is a route, not a ladder. Mine starts by the Vltava and currently passes through Stockholm Central Station.

Prague

Departure

  1. Until 2007

    Charles University three degrees

    MSc in Economics and Finance, plus bachelor's degrees in economics and international relations. Later a data science programme at London Business School. Detours along the way: Frankfurt, Nice, and a high school in Kansas.

  2. 2007–2010

    Mint Investments Investment Analyst, then Business Developer

    Real estate from the deal side. I prepared land acquisitions for the investment committee, took development projects from purchase to zoning, and contributed to the turnaround of a struggling shopping centre in Slovakia. Along the way I built the firm's investment model.

  3. 2010–2011

    Beleon Founder & CEO

    My own company: energy-saving LED lighting with financing included, sourced directly from manufacturers in Southeast Asia. It taught me every lesson about cash flow you can only learn with your own money.

Stockholm

Current station

  1. 2012–2017

    Unibail-Rodamco Analyst, then Asset Manager

    Moved country, changed language, kept the spreadsheet. First I rebuilt the reporting and forecasting, then I looked after a portfolio of office buildings: budgets, leasing and tenant negotiations.

  2. 2017–now

    Jernhusen Senior Business Controller, then Head of Analytics

    The company behind Sweden's railway stations, and the longest stop so far. In autumn 2020 I took over the property valuation, deep in the pandemic and just before interest rates exploded. The portfolio was worth SEK 18 billion then. Today it is 29.

    Valuing a portfolio leads straight to the next question, and I work on that too: which properties to keep, which to sell and which to develop. One of them is Centralstaden Stockholm, the most exciting of Sweden's urban development projects.

    Numbers are only useful if people use them, so I built the company's Power BI platform from scratch. Today 70% of my colleagues use it regularly. Lately I also build my own analysis tools with AI.
